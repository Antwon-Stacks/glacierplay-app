GlacierPlay Rewards Program
Terms & Conditions

1. PROGRAM OVERVIEW
The GlacierPlay Rewards Program is a promotional engagement feature operating within the GlacierPlay application. Users accumulate promotional "Keys" through daily ad-gated check-ins and spend Keys to unlock virtual Mystery Chests or reserve NFT mint permits.

2. VIRTUAL ASSET STATUS
Keys, streak counters, and opened chest allocations are virtual off-chain ledger points only. They hold no fiat value, cannot be purchased, and cannot be exchanged for cash within the mobile application.

3. RECEIVE ADDRESS & PRIVACY
Users register an Ethereum Virtual Machine (EVM) public receive address ("0x...") to bind reward allocations off-chain. GlacierPlay collects zero Personally Identifiable Information (PII).

4. EXTERNAL CLAIMING & GAS FEES
Cryptographic token claims ($ICED) and NFT minting are executed strictly outside the mobile application on the external Polar Portal dApp. Users are strictly responsible for all network transaction gas fees.

5. ANTI-EXPLOIT POLICY
Bypassing daily limits, using automated scripts, or attempting rapid address-cycling exploits will result in immediate forfeiture of key balances and blacklisting of the registered address.
